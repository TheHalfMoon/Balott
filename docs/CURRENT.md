## Canonical position

The product, gameplay, fairness, ranked/integrity, bot/voice/AI, social/Real Table/152, architecture, competitive landscape, player-desire/sport-simulation doctrine, globalization contract, roadmap, execution method, and provenance plans are now present on `main`.

`docs/PLAYER_DESIRE_AND_SPORT_SIMULATION.md` requires Balott to digitize the reasons people love Baloot — mastery, autonomy, partnership, social ritual, rivalry, uncertainty, identity, and history — rather than merely digitizing cards and rules. It also records the FIFA/EA SPORTS FC translation model, Competitive Table vs Authentic Majlis, Balott DNA, Stay at Table/Keep Partner, and explicit rejection of packs/FOMO/pay-to-win/scripting patterns.

`docs/LOCALIZATION_AND_GLOBALIZATION.md` makes multilingual architecture a first-phase requirement. Arabic remains the cultural/product reference, while English, Urdu, French, Spanish, German, Simplified Chinese, Persian, and Portuguese are launch-target languages. Arabic, Urdu, and Persian are first-class RTL locales. User-facing implementation must remain language-neutral at the game-authority layer and native-quality at the presentation layer.

No product code has been implemented by this planning package.

## First implementation frontier

When implementation is explicitly authorized, begin with `P00 / G0001 — Repository/toolchain foundation` from `docs/ROADMAP.md`.

P00 must include the localization infrastructure required by `docs/LOCALIZATION_AND_GLOBALIZATION.md`: resource architecture, locale negotiation, pseudo-localization, bidi/RTL strategy, semantic message identifiers, standards-based formatting, and translation validation/CI direction. Do not defer these until after the table UI is built.

Do not jump directly to the UI, bot, voice, 152, clubs, or online service fleet. The dependency order exists to establish deterministic game truth and proof infrastructure before product breadth.
